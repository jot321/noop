# Audit, 21 October 2026: calculations, speed and screens

Read-only audit of `dev` at `a978ca1`. Three passes:

- a calculation review of `Packages/StrandAnalytics` and `Strand/Data/IntelligenceEngine.swift`, cross-checked against a private copy of the owner's database (June–July 2026 plus 20 Oct; WHOOP 5.0/MG);
- a UI performance review of `Strand/Screens`, `Strand/Liquid`, `Packages/StrandDesign` and `StrandiOS/App`;
- a screen walkthrough in the iOS Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro Max and iPad Pro 13", Debug, `--demo-screen` harness) against that same database copy, with cloud sync removed.

The database values that served as evidence are kept out of this file.

Tests at audit time: StrandAnalytics passed 1,015/1,015, WhoopStore 244/244 and WhoopProtocol 240/240. StrandTests passed 916 of 918; the 2 failures are the known `TodayExplainabilityTests` localisation-key failures.

## Status (2026-10-21)

Fixed on `dev`, with tests:
- A1: each night is scored against a baseline folded only from earlier nights, and on-device nights from before the pass window now seed it.
- A2: twinless edits count only on the day they end.
- A3, sleep V2: cycle prior capped below each stage's log-prior gap, plus per-channel absolute spread floors in the night's z-scores.
- A6: rMSSD uses only adjacent, arrival-ordered beats and skips time gaps.
- A12: step increments capped at 4 per elapsed second.
- A13: a day needs 200 HR samples of its own, and stale weekly Vitality/Body Age points are removed.
- A15: a post-sync rescore widens to the oldest unscored backfilled day, and a one-off full-history rescore runs on upgrade.

Effect on the owner's data (Simulator, real copy):
- the phantom 39-minute sleeps and the no-data day rows are gone;
- the backfilled day is scored;
- the first four nights are calibrating instead of scored;
- nightly HRV is 8–22% lower;
- steps are about 10–13% lower.

Not fixed, still open:
- **Sleep stages remain approximate on this strap.** After the fix, V2 gives 19–35% deep and 11–36% REM. The default V1 gives 0–8% deep and 3–10% REM. Typical adult shares are about 13–23% deep and 20–25% REM.
- A4, A5, A7–A11 and A14 are still open.

## 1. Calculations that show wrong numbers

| # | Finding | Where | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Recovery (Charge) is scored against a baseline that includes the night itself and later nights.** One baseline is folded over the whole 21-day pass, and every night is scored against it. So the first nights get a score with no prior baseline, and early scores shift as later nights arrive. The baseline also never sees more than 21 days, so the 14-night "trusted" state and the 14/21-night half-lives are cut short. | `IntelligenceEngine.swift:725-731`, `:804` | Score each day against a baseline folded only from earlier nights, read from persisted history, not the pass window. |
| A2 | **A hand-logged nap is added to every day without a detected night.** The manual sleep blocks aren't filtered by day, so one logged nap shows up as identical "sleep" on every night with no detected sleep. Sleep debt, Rest and Vitality then pick it up, and the Compare overlay flatlines. | `IntelligenceEngine.swift:1668-1671`, `SleepStageTotals.swift:490-495` | Pass only manual blocks whose end falls on that local day. |
| A3 | **Sleep staging V2 produces REM and deep sleep without evidence.** The REM clock prior (+1.0·c) beats the light-vs-REM prior gap (0.82) late in the night, so a featureless night comes out about 21% REM. Per-night z-scores (`:408-419`) force contrast inside any window. Real nights showed implausible REM or deep shares; one nap the strap itself scored mostly awake came out with deep and REM. | `SleepStagerV2.swift:369-370` | Turn V2 off by default or label it experimental. Cap the prior and require absolute evidence before assigning REM or deep. |
| A4 | **"HF power" and total power have no real units.** The band integral is normalised by 2σ², so 5 ms and 50 ms HF sine waves both give about 1.0. The Stress screen labels this value "HF power". LF/HF is unaffected, because the normalisation cancels in the ratio. | `HRVFreqDomain.swift:208-211`, `StressView.swift:378-383` | Use the unnormalised Lomb-Scargle (power/N), or scale so the integral equals the variance. |
| A5 | **The Baevsky stress index and the LF/HF tiles use the whole day's R-R.** SI is defined on short, stationary resting records. A whole-day SI reads several times lower than a 5-minute sleep window on the same day. | `StressView.swift:95-122`, `AICoach.swift:536-541` | Compute on 5-minute still windows and show the latest or the median. |
| A6 | **RMSSD is inflated.** Beats within the same second are sorted by value instead of arrival order, and differences are taken across deleted beats and across time gaps. Nightly averages read about 7–18% high. Daytime hourly RMSSD on sparse R-R reads physiologically impossible values, and these feed DaytimeStress. | `WhoopStore/Reads.swift:105` (`ORDER BY ts ASC, rrMs ASC`), `HRVAnalyzer.swift:84-92`, `:117-137`, `DaytimeStress.swift:183-194` | Order by rowid or store a beat sequence; difference only beats that are adjacent in time. |
| A7 | **Respiratory rate moves in whole steps of about 1 bpm** (60 / median breath interval on a 4 Hz grid). With the 0.5 spread floor, a single step is z 1.6, and two steps fire the illness signal. | `SleepStager.swift:1392-1399` | Use parabolic peak interpolation or the spectral peak. |
| A8 | **The Rest term in recovery is centred at 0.85,** a value tuned for raw sleep efficiency, but the term now reads the Rest composite. That composite is capped near 95, because sleep need (8 h) and consistency (0.5) are never personalised. The result biases Charge down by about 4 points. | `RecoveryScorer.swift:63-65`, `:236-238`, `IntelligenceEngine.swift:595`, `:857`, `:1563` | Re-centre on the composite's own distribution, or z-score Rest against the personal baseline. |
| A9 | **ACWR is biased upward and uses log-scale Effort.** The 28-day chronic window comes from a 21-day pass, so at least 7 days are zero-filled and a steady load reads 1.33 ("building"). The 1.3/1.5 literature bands assume linear load. | `IntelligenceEngine.swift:1192-1199`, `StrainScorer.swift:223-227` | Run ACWR on TRIMP over full history, optionally as EWMA (Williams 2017). |
| A10 | **The skin-temperature curve and sleep HR curve include strap warm-up** (amplitudes of several °C from the first minutes on the wrist). The "core temperature" wording is backwards: wrist skin temperature rises at sleep onset. | `IntelligenceEngine.swift:1002-1004`, ThermoCurveEngine | Require concurrent HR, and drop the first 20–30 minutes after the strap goes on. |
| A11 | **HRV by stage** joins every segment of a stage into one series, and computes LF/HF over 6–8 h records. Wake ends up the highest-RMSSD stage on every night. | `HRVByStage.swift:78`, `:90` | Compute per 5-minute window. |
| A12 | **Steps are inflated by about 25–35%** by impossible 1-second counter jumps of 5 or more. Steps feed Vitality, Body Age and Fitness Age. | `AnalyticsEngine.swift:557-571` | Cap each 1-second delta at about 4. |
| A13 | **Days with no data still get rows.** The guard checks the 54 h read window, not the day's own data. metricSeries is upsert-only, so stale weekly rows survive. | `IntelligenceEngine.swift:505`, `MetricSeriesStore` | Gate on the day's own HR, and delete stale rows. |
| A14 | **`activeKcalEst` is total burn over worn time, not active burn.** Day calories count 1 s per sample, so they undercount on mixed-cadence days. | `WorkoutDetector.swift:490-502` | Rename the field or subtract resting burn; weight samples by elapsed time. |
| A15 | **Backfilled days older than the 21-day rescore window are never scored.** After a long gap, the strap's history arrives but those days get no metrics. | `IntelligenceEngine.swift:341` (window) | When backfill lands days outside the window, run a one-off pass over those days. |

### Fragile

- **Resting HR hardening is unused.** Daily RHR comes from `SleepStager.swift:1887-1901` with no minimum-sample or plausibility check, so the #686 hardening in `RecoveryScorer.swift:103-133` never runs. `sessionAvgHRV` counts windows that hold only 2 beats.
- **Workout and live strain assume resting HR 60** (`AppModel.swift:790`, `:840`; `Repository.swift:2150`; `ManualWorkoutRescore.swift:50`), while the daily score uses the sleep RHR.
- **Mixed-cadence zone minutes undercount:** a 30-s-cadence bout counts as 1 s per sample (`HRZones.swift:160-168`).
- **DFA α1:**
  - After a real step change above 5%, the reference never updates, so the series is held and the engine returns nil.
  - It computes one α1 per whole workout, not per 2-minute window.
  - It joins across gaps (`DFAAlpha1Engine.swift:85-96`).
- **Early baselines start at the spread floor** with a 21-night half-life, so early z-scores are about 1.3× too large (`Baselines.swift:150`, `:254`).
- **One fixed time-zone offset is applied to all history** (`IntelligenceEngine.swift:385`, `:1691`). Correct while at home; a pass run abroad re-keys every day.
- **Bedtimes before midnight can be clipped** (`AnalyticsEngine.swift:346-349`).
- **The 200k-row read limit** has only about 3% headroom (`Reads.swift:27-41`).
- **NaN handling:**
  - One NaN silently drops a whole metricSeries batch (`MetricSeriesStore.swift:31-45`).
  - 144 `Int(x.rounded())` call sites in the UI would crash on NaN. None are stored today.
- **The illness baseline counts rows, not calendar days** (`AppModel.swift:1499`).

### Cosmetic

- Banister TRIMP for women uses 0.64 instead of 0.86 (`StrainScorer.swift:68-70`). That path is unused.
- The "Edwards" zones use %HRR, not %HRmax (`StrainScorer.swift:73-75`). That is defensible, but it should be documented.
- The recalibration epoch check parses local day keys as UTC (`Baselines.swift:320-330`).
- The device table says "WHOOP 4.0" for a 5/MG.
- `pairedDevice` lists SpO2 although there are no SpO2 samples.
- The rrInterval primary key drops repeated equal beats in the same second.

### Not supportable on a WHOOP 5.0/MG with this data

Hide these, or label them clearly, rather than showing fake precision:

- **SpO2 and the apnea screen:** there are no samples. If data ever arrives, the method is unsound anyway:
  - peak-to-peak AC from 1 Hz samples;
  - an uncalibrated 110−25R curve;
  - an ad-hoc AHI with clinical labels.
- **Respiration:** there are no resp samples, so the rate is an RSA estimate (see A7).
- **Daytime HRV and stress:** built on very sparse R-R coverage.
- **Sleep stages from V2:** see A3.
- **Vitality, Body Age and Fitness Age to 0.1 year:** built on inflated steps and few days.

## 2. Why the UI lags

Charts and SQLite are not the cause. Charts are downsampled to about 400 points (`TrendChart.swift:128-135`), and the store runs on its own actor.

1. **Today reloads all history on every day swipe and refresh, on the main actor.**
   - `.task(id: "\(repo.refreshSeq)-\(selectedDayOffset)")` re-runs `load()` (`LiquidTodayView.swift:293`).
   - `load()` calls:
     - `repo.workoutRows()` with `days: 4000` (`Repository.swift:2033`), plus an O(n²) cross-source dedup (`WorkoutSource.swift:225-237`) and up to 300 HR-trace reads. Today only uses `.first` and `.count`.
     - four `exploreSeries` calls, each of which merges and sorts the full history on `@MainActor` (`Repository.swift:1854-1869`).
2. **The Liquid canvases never stop.**
   - The `LiquidThread` sparkline redraws at 60 fps (`LiquidPrimitives.swift:375`).
   - The sky redraws at 20 fps for star twinkle (`LiquidSky.swift:70`).
   - The three hero vessels run at 60 fps and rarely count as settled. A random nudge fires every 3.5–8.5 s (`LiquidCore.swift:248-253`), and hand tremor feeds the tilt.
   - Each vessel frame draws 4 wave paths, glints and 28 flecks. It converts `UIColor(Color)` every frame (`LiquidCore.swift:28-42`), bypassing `ColorComponentCache` and re-parsing hex.
   - Total at rest: roughly 100–260 Canvas redraws per second.
3. **Animation runs while hidden.**
   - Today keeps animating under its sheets and under other tabs.
   - CoreMotion samples at 60 Hz and isn't stopped on background (`LiquidCore.swift:93-112`). The app stays alive in the background for Bluetooth.
4. **Animated vessels on other screens, with no scroll gate:** Sleep, Health, Explore, Workouts, Stress, Intelligence, Hydration and Breathe.
5. **The floating tab bar** stacks glass or material, a translucent fill, a gradient stroke and an 18 pt shadow over moving content (`RootTabView.swift:562-623`).
6. **Over-invalidation.**
   - Palette colours are computed `static var`s that rebuild a dynamic `UIColor` on every access (`Palette.swift:191-256`).
   - Every vessel or tube re-init allocates a `LiquidSim`.
   - Several screens observe `AppModel` or `LiveState` at the root, so they re-render on the 1 Hz HR update and on every log line: Settings, Workouts, DataSources and BackupSync.
   - Each log line runs 3 regex compiles and an O(5,000) `removeFirst` (`LiveState.swift:479-503`).
   - Two observers are never read (`LiquidTodayView.swift:21`, `RootTabView.swift:9`).
7. **Work done inside `body`.**
   - SleepView builds its model up to 3× per refresh.
   - Formatters are created inside `body`.
   - `YearHeatStrip` builds up to 4,000 non-lazy cells.
   - The Trends and Sleep "lazy" scaffolds wrap one eager VStack.

Size of the decorative layer: `Strand/Liquid` is 2,864 lines (980 of them engine). StrandDesign defines 70 public colour tokens (about 120 hex values), 14 gradients, 4 domain "worlds", 42 font sizes and 28 more `.system(size:)` sizes. `TimeOfDayBackground` and `TypicalRangeBar` have no callers. The classic `TodayView` (4,765 lines) survives only as a fallback.

## 4. Suggested order of work

1. **Wrong numbers first, a small and contained change set:** A2, A1, A6, A12, A13, A15, then A3 (V2 off or experimental), A5/A4 (stress tiles), A7 and A8. Each gets a regression test against a synthetic day.
2. **Speed quick wins, same look:**
   - Split Today's day-scoped loads from the history-wide ones. Replace `workoutRows()` there with "latest workout plus count".
   - Use the static sky, and drop the thread's glint.
   - Default `animated: false`, and stop animation when a sheet is up, the app is inactive, or the screen is off-screen. Release CoreMotion on background.
   - Compute palette RGB once.
   - Remove unused observers, move hot `model`/`live` reads into leaf views, use static formatters, and use a ring-buffer log.
3. **Minimal redesign:**
   - One accent colour plus neutrals, system text styles, flat cards with no shadow or blur, static score rings, a flat background and the native tab bar.
   - Delete `Strand/Liquid`, the sky, the classic Today and the dead StrandDesign scenic code.
   - Fix the screen issues above as each screen moves over.
